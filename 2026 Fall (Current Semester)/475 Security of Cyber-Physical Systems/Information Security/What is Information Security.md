A condition that results from the establishment and maintenance of protective measures that enable an enterprise to perform its mission or critical functions despite risks posed by threats to its use of information systems 

[[Protective measures]] may involve a combination of:
	Deterrence
	Avoidance
	Prevention
	Detection
	Recovery
	Correction

There was more money stolen using Cyber Fraud this year than the entire history of bank robberies. Why is this the case?
	There really is no deterrent strong enough to prevent these types of things.
