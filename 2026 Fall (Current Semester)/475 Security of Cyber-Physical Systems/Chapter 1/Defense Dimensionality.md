The principle is that there are several layers of defense. If one layer gets compromised, then that doesn't mean everything is leaked. Just that layer, and anything that might be on that layer is leaked. More secure information should be deeper in the layers.

Defense-in-Depth is like protecting a single castle, defense-in-breadth is like coordinating defenses of multiple castles. Implies the development of security where systems or components work together to defend each other so that they will not be subject to a divide-and-conquer approach.

The intent is to avoid cascading failures
