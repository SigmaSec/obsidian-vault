Although consumers had opted in to share data with companies, an average of 48% of the over 2000 people Groopman and Etlinger interviewed were uncomfortable with the companies actually using their data.
	58% percent were uncomfortable with that data being sold.
	Only 20% of their survey participants felt that the benefits of their smart devices outweigh their privacy concerns.
		While the industry is rushing to make a hosts of devices smarter, they found that "adding a sensor to something does not magically endow it with value for this user, particular when weighed against the potential risk."
