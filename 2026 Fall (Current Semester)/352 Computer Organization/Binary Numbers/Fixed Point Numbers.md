So far, we have only talked about numbers that are evenly divisible by a whole number
Fixed point numbers allows us to represent fractional numbers on a computer
	... 10^2 10^1 10^0. 10^-1 10^-2
		10^-1 = 1/10 or 0.5 = 1/2
	... 2^2 2^1 2^0. 2^-1 2^-2 ...
		(0.1)(2) = 1/2
