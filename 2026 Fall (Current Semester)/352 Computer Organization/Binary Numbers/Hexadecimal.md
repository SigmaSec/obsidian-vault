Hexadecimal:
	base 16 numerical system
	For the numbers 0-9 nothing changes, but 10-15 letters A through F are used. 
		Each digit is equivalent to 4 bits, and 2 hex digits are equivalent to 8 bits (1 byte)
	To avoid ambiguity use the '0x' prefix. 
	For example: 
		101 does not equal 0x101, that is the equivalent to 257
