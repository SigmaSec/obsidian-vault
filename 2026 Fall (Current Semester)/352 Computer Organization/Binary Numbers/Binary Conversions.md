In binary, you can represent states by taking each bit to represent a power of 2, and take the sum to find the number. 
	For example, 11010 is short for: 
		1 * 2 ^4 + 1 * 2^3 + 0 * 2^2 +  1 * 2^1 + 0 * 2^0 = 16 + 8 + 2 = (101)(base 2) 
			To indicate a base 2 (binary) number:
			(101) (base)10

convert binary to decimal:
	1111 = 1 + 2 + 4 + 8 = 15
	0011 = 1 + 2 = 3
	1010 = 2 + 8 = 10

convert decimal to binary
	5 = 0101
