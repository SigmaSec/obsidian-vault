What does securing these systems mean? 
	Answer: Achieve confidentiality, integrity, and availability

How can we achieve this?
	Through mechanisms that security support:
		Identification, authentication, access control, accountability, and non-repudiation

non-repudiation - basically a digital signature
Three main goals - remember CIA, Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability.
Subgoals - remember AAA,  Authentication, access control, accountability
access control == authorization
